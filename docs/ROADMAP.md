# Solo delivery plan — start 22 Oct 2026, term 10 Nov 2026

**Hard constraint: 20 hours per week TOTAL across five independent courses.** Evenly spread, that is 4 h per course per week. The five accredited outlines describe 5 + 10 + 10 + 5 + 10 = **40 nominal hours/week** of course workload when simultaneous; therefore the available time is materially below combined nominal workload. Prioritise according to the actual timetable and ask faculty about solo/group rules. No plan here guarantees full SaaS completion by term start.

## Pre-term runway (Oct 22-Nov 9)
About 19 days / 2.7 weeks / roughly 54 available hours total. Proposed 10-11 hours per product over the runway in a balanced allocation.
- Shared **week A**: each project 4 h: unzip, audit, run starter, record data/legal risks.
- Shared **week B**: each project 4 h: one tested improvement to first vertical slice and academic study protocol.
- **Final pre-term partial week**: each project 2-3 h: frozen assessment map, latest source references, next session plan.

## Term weeks 1-2
Allocate first 20 h according to urgent assessments, with a baseline target of 4 h/project: syllabus reconciliation, real data feasibility, interview scheduling, P0 contracts.

## Term weeks 3-6
Academic baselines + first trained experiment (where required), Design Thinking primary research, week-4/6 assessed group/project evidence. Save methodology and experiment logs.

## Term weeks 7-10
Complete one end-to-end decision workflow per subject, execute ablations, collect reviewer feedback, address assessments. Release usable prototype evidence; do not chase commercial features.

## Term weeks 11-12 / assessment
Stabilise; freeze runs for reports, use actual screenshots/metrics, rehearse viva, complete reflection and presentations. Verify solo submission permission.

## Post-assessment commercial phase (per product)
Permissioned pilot -> auth and tenant separation -> integration -> reliable operations -> billing -> customer feedback -> paid trial. Prioritise at most one or two products commercially at once unless resourcing changes.

## Work session pattern (4 h)
0:20 read state/test; 0:35 scope and design; 2:00 implement; 0:40 test/document; 0:25 update project handoff. Log consumed hours and unfinished work accurately.
