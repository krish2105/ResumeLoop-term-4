# Two-week fieldwork sprint

Prepared 1 November 2026. This is the work that moves the Design Thinking marks; the software only makes it faster and
traceable. About **10 hours** over 14 days, inside the 4 hours a week this project gets. Instruments are in
[`research/STUDY_KIT.md`](../research/STUDY_KIT.md); the framework and references are in [`FRAMEWORK.md`](FRAMEWORK.md).

**Rules that do not bend:** adults only, voluntary, consent recorded before any note becomes data, no names in notes, no
real grades or messages, no invented participants or results. If faculty say something different from this plan, follow
faculty.

## Before day 1 (30 minutes, you)
1. Send the faculty questions from `/course` → Faculty questions (copy the draft into your own email). Ask about groupmates
   or solo permission, dates, the ethics process, required reflective frameworks and AI-use disclosure.
2. Wait for the ethics answer before recruiting. Fill the missing values in the study kit's information script
   (your contact, storage place, withdrawal contact, deletion date).
3. Pick the pilot course. It must be one you are allowed to share: for example, your own study group's plan for a unit,
   or a course whose coordinator agrees in writing. Never copy a course page you have no right to share.

## The sprint
| Day | Step | Time | App screen | What it produces for the rubric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Recruit 4 learners (at least one who recently missed a session) and 2 coordinators or tutors. Add each to the tracker with a code (P01…) and consent state | 45 min | `/studio/research` | Research relevance: who and why |
| 3–5 | 6 interviews of 25 min, plus a 15-min guided tour of how they catch up today. Capture live, tag moments, convert to notes only after consent | 4 h | `/studio/research` → `/studio/observe` | Raw qualitative evidence |
| 6 | Thematic analysis phases 1–4: code exact quotes (accept or reject each AI suggestion), cluster into themes, keep negative cases | 1.5 h | `/studio/empathy`, `/studio/synthesis` | Data and information analysis |
| 7 | Points of view and Hills from the themes, each linked to evidence; journey map of today's process | 45 min | `/studio/define`, `/studio/envision`, `/studio/journeys` | Understanding key issues |
| 8 | Co-design session, 60 min with 3–5 participants: Crazy 8s, one vote each, score three alternatives (LMS checklist, coordinator brief, ResumeLoop) | 1.5 h | `/studio/ideate` | Decision among alternatives; outcome E |
| 9 | Change the prototype only where the session pointed; freeze the test protocol | 1 h | `/studio/prototypes`, `/studio/experiments` | Iteration evidence |
| 10–11 | Usability round 1: 5 people, each does the same two tasks with their current way and with ResumeLoop (alternate the order) | 2 h | `/studio/experiments` | Feasibility evidence |
| 10–14 | Browser pilot: load the pilot course, give 3–5 learners a code each (L01…), collect progress files on day 12 and day 14 | 30 min of yours | `/coordinator`, learners use `/settings` | Real-use evidence for the MVP |
| 14 | Compile the evidence report; set story mode's counts from real data; write two journal entries | 1 h | `/report`, `/story`, `/reflect` | Presentation and the reflective paper |

Round 2 (another 5 people after fixing what round 1 found) is optional in the sprint and strong if time allows.

## Recruitment message (send it yourself, after the ethics answer)
> Hi, I'm studying how people get back on track after missing a class, for my Design Thinking unit at SP Jain. It's a
> 25-minute chat about your most recent catch-up, and you can skip any question or stop at any time. Taking part is
> voluntary and has nothing to do with grades. I won't record names. Would you be up for it this week?

## What to record, so the evidence holds up
- **Per interview:** participant code, date, protocol version, consent choices, then notes with quotes kept apart from
  your interpretation.
- **Per usability task:** success or not, seconds, wrong actions, help given, 1–7 ease rating. Keep failed tasks in the
  denominator.
- **Per pilot learner:** the progress files only (code, tasks done, version seen, question subjects). Download the CSV
  summary on day 14 and keep it with the evidence.
- **Your own reflection:** a dated entry after each step: what you expected, what surprised you, what you changed.
