# Business case and commercial validation — ResumeLoop

**Proposed ICP:** Cohort-based professional education provider; pay per active cohort or institution after evidence of reduced catch-up coordination.

## Interview research, before pricing
Interview 6-10 reachable people as a planning goal. Ask for a recent concrete instance of the job, current tools, frequency, impact of failure, access to relevant records, who decides on purchasing, onboarding restrictions and budget owner. Separate interesting feedback from actual evidence of intent to buy.

## Pilot charter
- Named sponsor, specific workflow, consent and dataset permission.
- Baseline measurement before introducing the product; pilot duration and mutually agreed success measures.
- Human approval on consequential actions; weekly feedback and failure log.
- Customer success outcomes must be observed and distinguished from model/simulation outcomes.

## Pricing hypotheses, not facts
Experiment with a small monthly workspace subscription and one relevant usage variable, plus bounded onboarding service. Do not publish arbitrary ROI or 'market-leading' claims. Track direct cloud/AI costs, time spent supporting uploads and acquired data rights.

## Customer conversion ladder
Problem interview -> prototype test -> authorised data proof-of-concept -> repeat usage -> pilot agreement -> paid contract -> second-tenant onboarding.

## Stop/pivot conditions
No access to necessary input, no repeat use, inability to get a trustworthy benchmark, licensing blockage, or a workflow cost larger than user-perceived benefit. Revisit audience or narrow features before building broad integrations.
