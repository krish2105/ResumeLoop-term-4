# Academic evidence and defence checklist

Prepare a reproducible bundle per subject (do not copy another course's study):
- Problem, specific user, current workflow, ethical considerations and why alternatives were rejected.
- Relevant studies and contrast to your actual contribution; full source attribution.
- Data description, rights, limitations, preprocessing and provenance.
- Method and justified benchmarks, actual environment/seeds/splits, chosen metrics.
- Measured outcomes in labelled figures plus confidence/uncertainty where appropriate.
- Error analysis: where does the system fail, whom could it harm, and how does it recover?
- Architectural explanation from uploaded data to usable result and human decision.
- Demonstration recording with genuine inputs and checks, no fabricated KPIs.
- Business viability: customer, buyer, measurable pain, actual validation and remaining assumptions.
- Honest limitations and future research; solo contribution diary.

## Suggested viva questions
1. Which unit learning outcome does your central experiment prove, and by what evidence?
2. Why is the simple baseline insufficient or sufficient?
3. What could cause leakage or false improvement in your dataset?
4. What part is a real observation versus a simulated assumption?
5. How would a customer notice incorrect output and recover?
6. Which competitor already addresses this, and why is your focused first user different?
7. What would it cost to deploy one more tenant, and how would you prevent cross-tenant access?
8. Which result surprised you and led you to change the product?
